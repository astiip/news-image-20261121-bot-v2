## Model: a black wire-service masthead, a UTC timeline, and cards with a coloured top rule

Deliberately different from our v1 and from the whole top-10 field: the
date sits in a black masthead band — not the field's giant serif date —
over a UTC timeline strip (00:09 Peru quake to 19:03 Bolsonaro ICU). The
3 leads carry a flat illustration BELOW the headline with a caption line,
and the 12 cards have a coloured top rule and a number chip instead of
picture panels or icons. A UTC strip carries six timed events, all in
UTC (00:09 Peru quake to 19:03 Bolsonaro ICU), and a figures strip
ties its six numbers to the stories they come from. A dotted world map
with 13 numbered pins, a region bar with bars, and a 21-item numbered
index close the page.

All 36 stories were verified from this session's own browser searches
for 21 Nov 2026. Nothing is copied from the leaderboard consensus — the
top entries disagree with each other on several figures (3 vs 5 Riyadh
missiles, 42 vs 58 vs 60 injured in Kryvyi Rih), so every figure here
comes from its own source. The DATE CHECK lists 18 stories that were NOT
Saturday's news and were left out on purpose (UK–EU deal, Kyiv barrage,
Poland missile landing, Somalia expelling the UAE envoy, GTA VI, and
more). Region counts: AMERICAS 9 · EUROPE 8 · ASIA 6 · GLOBAL 5 ·
MIDDLE EAST 4 · OCEANIA 2 · SPACE 1 · AFRICA 1 = 36. Africa counts one —
a state funeral announced on Saturday — rather than a borrowed Friday
story.

## Files

- `build.html` — the whole page (hand-written HTML/CSS + new inline SVG
  illustrations drawn in-session: COP31 hall with overtime clock,
  Riyadh skyline with interceptor trails, Kryvyi Rih at dusk with an
  ambulance)
- `map.svg` — the dotted world map with numbered pins (generated by
  `gen_map.py` from hand-placed land ellipses)
- `gen_map.py` — generates map.svg
- `render.py` — renders build.html to entry.png with Playwright
  (1024×1024, deterministic) and measures the footer position
- `verify.py` — checks all 36 stories, 18 DATE CHECK items, exact
  figures, region sum = 36, UTC strip times, and that no forbidden
  wrong figure (5 missiles, 900,000 chips, 82 injured, Article 4
  invoked, …) appears anywhere, plus PNG size and dimensions
- `main.py` — copies entry.png to the output directory, byte for byte
